How We Choose Technology

AI isn't always the answer.

We use AI where it's genuinely useful: classification, extraction, summarisation, interpretation. For everything else, we use simpler, more reliable automation.

The right solution is the one that solves your problem reliably, not the one that looks most impressive on a slide deck.

What We Don't Automate
✖

Decisions requiring professional judgment

✖

Processes where automation would introduce unacceptable risk

✖

Automation that costs more than the labour it replaces

✖

Decisions requiring human professional, legal, or ethical judgment

Automation isn't about removing humans. It's about removing unnecessary human effort.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions we often hear

What types of business processes can you automate?

Lead processing, customer support, document handling, reporting, data entry, notifications, CRM workflows, scheduled operations, and anything else that involves moving information between systems.

Do I need to replace my existing software?

Usually not. We work with what you already have: CRMs, spreadsheets, email, databases. We connect them rather than replacing them.

When should a business use AI instead of traditional automation?

When the task involves interpretation, classification, extraction, summarisation, or context-dependent responses. If a simple rule works, we use a simple rule.

Will humans still be involved in automated workflows?

Yes. We build systems that know when to ask for help. Human approval belongs where mistakes are expensive.

How do you handle failures and errors?

Every automation we build has a failure strategy. Things break. We make sure they break gracefully and alert someone when they do.
