Automated Schedule Management System

Problem
Keeping a schedule synchronized across a spreadsheet, calendar, and daily reminders required manual upkeep.

System
The automation reads the schedule, creates calendar events and reminders, removes completed events automatically, and sends a daily summary through Telegram at 5:00 AM.

Result
One schedule becomes the source of truth while the surrounding administrative work happens automatically.
