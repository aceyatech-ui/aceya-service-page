Case Study

AI Lead Finder & Outreach

n8n Tavily Hunter Gemini Gmail Google Sheets APIs

Project type: Independent build

01. Problem

Finding qualified prospects and reaching out to them manually does not scale.

Lead generation normally involves searching for potential companies, researching each prospect, finding contact details, deciding whether the lead is relevant, writing an appropriate message, and finally sending or preparing the outreach.

Repeating that process lead by lead creates a large amount of repetitive work and makes consistent personalisation difficult.

02. Objective

Build an automated pipeline that finds prospects and takes them from discovery to outreach.

The objective was to create a system that could discover relevant companies, extract useful information, find contact details, evaluate the available data, generate personalised outreach, and prepare the resulting emails automatically.

03. Architecture

Lead discovery, enrichment, personalisation, and outreach in one pipeline.

The workflow connects multiple APIs and automation stages so that a discovered lead can continue through the pipeline without requiring manual processing at every step.

05. Implementation

What was built

  • Automated lead discovery using Tavily
  • Lead extraction and parsing from search results
  • Lead deduplication and storage
  • Hunter integration for contact enrichment
  • Best-email selection from available contact data
  • Gemini-powered personalised outreach generation
  • Structured output handling for generated emails
  • Gmail integration for outreach preparation
  • Google Sheets logging and lead status tracking
06. Tradeoffs

What worked, what didn't, and what changed.

  • AI personalisation vs reliability
    AI produced more contextual messages, but generated output required validation and fallback handling.
  • Personalisation vs speed
    Generating a message for every lead adds processing time. For higher-volume outreach, a simpler predefined approach can be more efficient.
  • Multiple APIs vs simplicity
    Connecting search, enrichment, AI, email, and storage creates a powerful system, but every additional dependency introduces another potential failure point.
  • Automation vs control
    Fully autonomous outreach is convenient, but keeping a human review step can provide better control over sensitive or high-value prospects.
07. Result

A complete lead-to-outreach automation pipeline.

The system can move from prospect discovery to contact enrichment, personalised message generation, and email preparation without requiring each lead to be manually researched and written from scratch.

The build also exposed an important practical lesson: automation does not automatically mean that every step should be fully autonomous. For volume outreach, reliability and consistency can be more valuable than maximum personalisation.

08. What I Learned
  • Lead generation becomes significantly more useful when discovery and outreach are connected.
  • AI-generated output needs validation and fallback paths.
  • Every external API adds capability but also adds another dependency to monitor.
  • The best automation is not necessarily the most complicated automation.
