Case Study

AI Lead Finder

AI Automation n8n Lead Generation APIs Gemini Google Sheets

Project type: Independent build

01. Problem

Finding good prospects manually is repetitive, slow, and difficult to scale.

Traditional lead generation often requires searching for companies, checking their websites, identifying relevant contacts, finding professional email addresses, researching the company, and then writing a personalised message for every prospect.

The work is valuable, but much of the process is repetitive enough to be handled by automation.

02. Objective

Build an end-to-end system that finds leads and prepares personalised outreach.

The goal was to automate the path from discovering potential companies to producing useful, personalised outreach while keeping the resulting lead data structured and reviewable.

Instead of treating lead generation and personalisation as separate tasks, the workflow connects them into one pipeline.

03. Architecture

Discovery → enrichment → intelligence → outreach.

The workflow moves a lead through several stages before it reaches the final outreach step.

01 / DISCOVERY

Find potential companies

Search and extraction tools identify companies that match the target criteria.

02 / ENRICHMENT

Research the lead

Company information is collected and relevant contacts are identified using enrichment and contact discovery services.

03 / AI INTELLIGENCE

Understand the context

AI evaluates the available context and generates a relevant subject and personalised outreach message.

04 / OUTPUT

Prepare the outreach

The resulting lead, contact information, and outreach content are structured for Gmail and Google Sheets.

05. Implementation

What was built.

  • Automated company discovery and web research.
  • Lead extraction, parsing, and structured storage.
  • Deduplication and company-level enrichment.
  • Contact discovery and professional email identification.
  • AI context analysis for determining useful personalisation.
  • AI-generated personalised subject lines and outreach messages.
  • Structured lead tracking through Google Sheets.
  • Gmail preparation for the resulting outreach.
06. AI Layer

AI is used for context, not just text generation.

The AI layer receives information gathered during the research and enrichment stages before generating the outreach. This gives the message actual company and contact context instead of relying on generic templates.

The system also produces structured output so the generated information can move cleanly into the rest of the workflow.

07. Tradeoffs

Key decisions and compromises.

  • Automation vs review — the system automates research and preparation while keeping the resulting lead data visible for review.
  • Broad discovery vs lead quality — finding more companies is less useful if enrichment and qualification cannot keep up.
  • AI flexibility vs structured output — AI is given room to reason about context, while its output is constrained into fields the workflow can reliably process.
  • Multiple APIs — external services make enrichment much more capable but also introduce dependencies, limits, and potential failure points.
08. Result

A complete automated path from prospect discovery to personalised outreach.

The system can discover potential companies, enrich their information, identify relevant contacts, evaluate available context, and generate personalised outreach for the resulting leads.

Lead information and workflow status remain structured in Google Sheets, while the generated outreach can be prepared for Gmail.

09. What I Learned
  • Lead generation becomes much more useful when discovery, enrichment, and outreach are treated as one system.
  • AI personalisation is significantly more useful when the model receives real research context before generating a message.
  • Structured intermediate data makes complex automation workflows easier to debug and maintain.
  • External APIs provide powerful capabilities but need careful handling because every dependency can become a failure point.
