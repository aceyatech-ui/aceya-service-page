Case Study

AI-Powered Lead Outreach

n8n Gemini Hunter Tavily Gmail APIs

Project type: Independent build

01. Problem

Manual lead research and email writing at scale was slow and repetitive.

Finding relevant leads, researching their companies, and writing personalised emails took hours. There was no way to scale the process without hiring a team or sacrificing quality.

02. Objective

Build a fully autonomous outreach engine that discovers leads, enriches contact details, generates personalised emails, and creates drafts — all without manual input.

The system should handle the entire pipeline: lead discovery, enrichment, personalisation, draft creation, and logging.

03. Architecture

Four-stage autonomous pipeline

The workflow runs end-to-end with zero manual intervention.

STAGE 1: LEAD DISCOVERY
→ Schedule/Manual Trigger
→ HTTP Request (Tavily)
→ Content Extractor
→ Parser
STAGE 2: ENRICHMENT
→ Lead Saver
→ Deduplication
→ Hunter
→ Best Email Picker
STAGE 3: AI GENERATION
→ Description Checker
→ AI Agent (Gemini)
→ Structured Output Parser
→ Merge
STAGE 4: DRAFT & LOG
→ Email Parser
→ Email Preparer
→ Gmail Draft
→ Google Sheets (Log)

05. Implementation

What was built

  • Tavily integration for automated lead discovery
  • Hunter integration for email enrichment
  • Gemini AI agent for personalised email generation
  • Structured output parser for guaranteed JSON response
  • Deduplication to prevent duplicate outreach
  • Gmail draft creation and Google Sheets logging
06. Tradeoffs

Key decisions and compromises

  • AI reliability — Gemini sometimes failed to produce valid JSON, causing fallback issues
  • Speed vs personalisation — AI generation took 10-20 seconds per lead; faster but less personalised approach was eventually chosen
  • Cost — each AI call costs a small amount, adding up at scale
  • Maintenance — complex workflows require more debugging and monitoring
07. Result

A fully autonomous outreach engine that finds, enriches, writes, and drafts.

The system discovers leads, finds emails, generates personalised messages, creates Gmail drafts, and logs everything — all without manual input.

The system was eventually replaced with a simpler, more reliable pre-written approach for volume sending.

A hybrid approach (AI for some leads, pre-written for others) would be the sweet spot.

08. What I Learned
  • AI is powerful but needs fallbacks for reliability
  • Structured output parsing helps but doesn't eliminate AI failures
  • Speed and reliability often matter more than personalisation in volume outreach
