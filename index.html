How We Think

Engineering principles behind our systems

We do not automate for the sake of adding technology. We choose the simplest reliable approach that solves the underlying business problem.

01

AI is not always the answer.

We use AI when interpretation or context makes it useful. For straightforward tasks, simpler automation can be more reliable.

02

Automation should reduce complexity.

A good system removes repetitive work without creating a new layer of unnecessary operational overhead.

03

Human approval matters when mistakes are costly.

Important decisions can include human review instead of forcing every workflow into full autonomy.

04

Security belongs in the architecture.

Access control, data handling, authentication, and security considerations should be part of the system from the beginning.

05

Every automation needs a failure strategy.

Systems should validate inputs, handle failures, record useful context, and alert the right people when something goes wrong.
